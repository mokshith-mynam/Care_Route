# Open Product Questions

Resolve these through interviews and technical review before expanding scope.

1. Which health-worker workflow is most painful today: follow-up scheduling, missed visits, referral outcomes, or workload visibility?
2. Who is the operational owner for unresolved cases?
3. What does “completed” mean for a follow-up versus a referral?
4. How should rescheduling and duplicate referrals be handled?
5. Are workers shared across facilities, and what data may cross facility boundaries?
6. What connectivity constraints exist in the target setting?
7. Which language(s) and accessibility needs must be supported?
8. Who would purchase and maintain the product, and what value metric matters?
9. What legal/privacy, consent, and clinical governance requirements apply to a pilot?
10. Which notification channels are approved, and what content may they reveal?
